Welcome to New Peony

A Legacy of Kashmiri
Hospitality on Water

Nestled on the serene waters of Nigeen Lake, New Peony Group of Houseboats offers an unparalleled experience of traditional Kashmiri hospitality blended with modern luxury. Our heritage houseboats, adorned with exquisite hand-carved walnut wood and traditional Kashmiri textiles, are floating palaces that tell the story of Kashmir's rich cultural heritage.

Each moment spent aboard is a journey through time — from the intricate woodwork on our ornate ceilings to the handwoven carpets beneath your feet, every detail speaks of artistry passed down through generations.

The Experience

Life on the
Serene Waters

Nigeen Lake, often called the "Jewel in the Ring," is one of Kashmir's most pristine and tranquil lakes. Away from the bustle of Dal Lake, Nigeen offers a more intimate, peaceful experience — where the only sounds are the gentle lapping of water and birdsong at dawn.
